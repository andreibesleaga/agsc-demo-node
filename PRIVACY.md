This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from any other host. Its scripts are all served from this node, and none of them sends anything anywhere: the compose page, which works in your browser and uploads nothing, the in-page tools on item pages, which answer an assistant running in your own browser from this node's own published files; and the theme control on every page. If you pick light or dark, that choice is kept in your browser's local storage under the one key `agsc-theme`; it is never sent anywhere, choosing Auto removes it, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes it too. It collects no personal data.

It does publish some. Every pattern names the person accountable for it, as the identifier `human:andreibesleaga`, in its page, its provenance record and the machine-readable views. The patterns cite other people's work by author, title and year, as a bibliography does. This node takes no outside contributions: its source repository is not public.

**Why.** The names are published so that every statement on this node can be traced to a person who answers for it — that is what the whole project is about. The footing for publishing them is the operator's own interest in running an accountable, attributable public record of technical work, and the public interest in being able to check who said what. Anyone named may object, and what happens then is set out below. Nothing is used for advertising, for profiling, or for any purpose other than the one stated here.

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. To deliver and protect it, Cloudflare processes the technical data of each request, such as the IP address, the requested address and the user agent, under its own privacy policy and its own retention periods. The operator runs no server and receives no per-request logs; what Cloudflare shows the operator is aggregate, such as how many requests the site served, from which countries and with which status codes. The source of this node is kept in a private repository on GitHub; nothing from it is published except what this site shows. Cloudflare and GitHub are companies based in the United States and may handle this data outside the European Union; both say that they rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for such transfers. No decision about anyone is made automatically.

Retention: the git history of this node's repository is the only record the operator keeps; no request logs are kept by the operator.

**Your rights.** If personal data about you is published here, you may ask to see what there is, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to have its use restricted, and to object to it being published at all. Use the contact route below and you will get an answer. One limit has to be said honestly: the history of this node's repository cannot be rewritten without destroying the record that makes this work checkable, so an erasure request against a name that is already in that history will usually be answered by removing the name from the site and from every export, and by explaining why the history itself stays. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (dataprotection.ro), or to the authority where you live.

Some of the names published here — the authors of the work the items cite — were never contacted, because there are too many of them for that to be reasonable. This notice is how they are told. They have the same rights, and the same contact route.
