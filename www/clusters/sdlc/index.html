Building software with agents: phases, approval gates and reviews.

Members

  • Human-Only Merge Gate: An agent works on its own branch and cannot approve or merge; a named human decides what reaches the main branch.
  • Plan Approval Gate: The agent researches and proposes a plan without editing source; a person approves, changes or rejects it before edits start.
