Privacy

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, and the pages it builds load nothing from any other host. Its scripts are all served from this node, and none of them sends anything anywhere: the compose page, which works in your browser and uploads nothing, the in-page tools on item pages, which answer an assistant running in your own browser from this node's own published files; and the theme control on every page. If you pick light or dark, that choice is kept in your browser's local storage under the one key agsc-theme; it is never sent anywhere, choosing Auto removes it, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes it too. It collects no personal data.

It does publish some. Every pattern names the person accountable for it, as the identifier human:andreibesleaga, in its page, its provenance record and the machine-readable views. The patterns cite other people's work by author, title and year, as a bibliography does. Contributions to this node arrive as pull requests on the public repository agsc-demo-node, which mirrors the published items and their sources; a contributor's name, the address they commit under and the sign-off line this project's specification requires become part of that repository's public history on GitHub — not of this site.

Why. The names are published so that every statement on this node can be traced to a person who answers for it — that is what the whole project is about — and so that contributions can be attributed to the people who made them. The footing for publishing them is the operator's own interest in running an accountable, attributable public record of technical work, and the public interest in being able to check who said what. Anyone named may object, and what happens then is set out below. Nothing is used for advertising, for profiling, or for any purpose other than the one stated here.

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. To deliver and protect it, Cloudflare processes the technical data of each request, such as the IP address, the requested address and the user agent, under its own privacy policy and its own retention periods. Cloudflare can also add features of its own to what it serves, which are not in the files this site builds: a bot-detection script that it injects into pages, a Network Error Logging header (NEL, with Report-To) that asks your browser to report failed connections to Cloudflare at a.nel.cloudflare.com, and a speculation rules header that lets your browser load a linked page before you open it. Where they are switched on, they belong to Cloudflare and work under its policy. The operator runs no server and receives no per-request logs; what Cloudflare shows the operator is aggregate, such as how many requests the site served, from which countries and with which status codes. The maintainer's working copy of this node, with its drafts, is a private repository on GitHub; the published items and their sources are mirrored in the public repository agsc-demo-node, whose history — including a contributor's name, the address they commit under and their sign-off line — is held and published by GitHub under its own terms. Cloudflare and GitHub are companies based in the United States and may handle this data outside the European Union; both say that they rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for such transfers. No decision about anyone is made automatically.

Retention: the git history of this node's repositories is the only record the operator keeps; no request logs are kept by the operator.
