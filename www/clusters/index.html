Clusters

SummaryEvery published cluster of this node.

  • Advanced Architectures (1 item): Larger agent systems built by combining simpler patterns, such as a research system with a lead agent and parallel searchers.
  • Anti-Patterns (1 item): Common mistakes in agent systems, how to recognise them, and how to avoid them.
  • Classical Foundations (2 items): Established software patterns, such as the saga, applied to agent workflows.
  • Cognitive & Behavioral (5 items): Ideas from cognitive science, such as self-monitoring, turned into agent patterns.
  • Data & Retrieval (1 item): How agents find and use the right information, and how to measure whether they did.
  • Ethics & Sustainability (2 items): Human control over autonomous systems, and their effects on people and the environment.
  • Evaluation & Testing (7 items): How to test agents and measure whether they work.
  • Foundational (7 items): The basic building blocks of agents, such as loops, tools and ways of combining model calls.
  • Future Research (1 item): Open research questions about agents, with what is known and what is not.
  • Governance & Accountability (1 item): Records, rules and checks that make agent actions accountable.
  • Memory & Cognition (1 item): How agents remember, forget and turn past work into reusable skills.
  • Operations & Reliability (4 items): Running agents in production, including handling failures, controlling cost and watching behaviour.
  • Product Practice (4 items): Product decisions for agents, such as how much an agent may do on its own.
  • Protocols & Interop (1 item): Shared formats and addresses that let agents, tools and organisations work together.
  • Reasoning & Decision (1 item): How agents set goals, decide what to do next and know when to stop.
  • Reference Architectures (1 item): Complete agent designs that combine several patterns into one working system.
  • Agentic SDLC (2 items): Building software with agents through phases, approval gates and reviews.
  • Security (5 items): Threats to agents, and the designs that defend against them.
  • Specs & Requirements (1 item): How to state what an agent should do when its behaviour is not fully predictable.
  • Multi-Agent Topologies (2 items): Ways for several agents to work together, such as a shared workspace or a hierarchy.
  • Upkeep (6 items): The node's own work on one board: the decision on card length, the card format, the gate and the open tasks.
