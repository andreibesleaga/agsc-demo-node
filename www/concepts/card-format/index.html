Specification

A card has an intent, the forces, the structure, the trade-offs and its related cards. Every claim names a public source. The text is about 60 words.

Relations

An outgoing relation is stated by this item; an incoming one is a backlink, stated by the other item. Each meaning reads from the item that states the relation towards its target.
